02 — Landscape

Competitive
Landscape

Subspace's direct competitive set in India is thin. Ranks #1 among 7 active competitors. No existing player replicates the Negotiate API or the group-sharing mechanic at scale.

Company Core Offering Funding Key Gap vs Subspace

Rocket Money (US) Subscription mgmt + cancellation Acquired by Rocket US-only, no group sharing, no India ops
The Structural Moat
What Subspace has that no competitor replicates: the Negotiate API — an automated price negotiation layer that plugs into brand APIs and extracts the best rate on behalf of the user. Building this requires direct brand relationships, years of price elasticity data, and significant engineering investment. The group-sharing mechanic also has genuine network effects: more admins → more groups → more savings → more members. The real competitive risk is not from existing Indian startups — it's from PhonePe, Paytm, or Google Pay adding subscription management to their existing fintech apps.

04 — The 5 Feedbacks

Non-Obvious
Observations

Each structured as Observed → Evidence & Analysis → Ship Instead. Spanning 4 pillars: UX (×2), Features, GTM, and Competitor Analysis.

01
UX
Onboarding Captures No Identity — The Platform Calls Every New User "User"
UX
Observed
After completing signup, the Profile screen shows: Full Name — "User", Username — "Not set", Email — "Not set". The onboarding flow accepted a phone number and location — and considered setup complete.
Evidence & Analysis
Every transaction requires identity — and the platform has none. Play Store, March 2026: "admin never replied." April 2026: "Groups will not be listed all of a sudden." These are trust failures from an identity-free design. Deloitte 2025: 38% leave mid-onboarding if it takes too long — Subspace avoids drop-off by asking nothing.
Ship Instead
3-step progressive onboarding: Step 1 — Phone OTP (done). Step 2 — First name + avatar (30 sec). Step 3 — Intent question: "What brings you to Subspace?" [Save OTT / Share / Rent / Explore]. Route immediately to the relevant surface.
02
Features
149+ Netflix Groups — Zero Trust Signals to Distinguish Any of Them
Features
Observed
Netflix shows 149+ groups at Rs.163/device. JioHotstar 42+ groups, Prime Video 36+ groups. Every group card shows identical metadata. No admin rating, no renewal rate, no "last active" timestamp, no dispute history.
Evidence & Analysis
Two simultaneous market failures: adverse selection (users default to price, not reliability) and good-admin under-investment (reliable admins can't signal quality or charge a premium). Airbnb solved this in 2012 with two-way ratings. Uber solved it with driver scores.
Ship Instead
Admin Trust Score on every group card. Inputs: renewal rate, response time, dispute rate, transaction count, account age. Display: 5-star or tiered badge (New / Verified / Trusted / Top Admin). "Top Admin" tier earns 15% revenue share vs standard 10% — flywheel incentive.
03
GTM
Credit Card Upsell Served to Cold Users Before the Product Has Done Anything for Them
GTM & ICPs
Observed
"Add your credit card & get personalised offers" appears at T=60 seconds. Before any subscription, transaction, or value delivery. Directly below: "No Active Subscriptions."
Evidence & Analysis
Trust-timing mismatch. 2026 fintech UX research: "The biggest onboarding mistake isn't too many steps — it's asking for trust before earning it." Progressive disclosure is standard practice at Stripe, Revolut, CRED. Core ICP (18–35) is alert to data asks.
Ship Instead
Gate behind a first-value milestone. Trigger after: (a) joining a group, (b) buying a gift card, or (c) saving Rs.100+. New copy: "You saved Rs.X this month. Connect your card — we'll find you Rs.Y more." Same feature, moment of maximum trust.
04
Competitor
AI Tools Category Absent — The Fastest-Growing, Highest-Savings Subscription Segment
Competitor Analysis
Observed
Explore lists Google Gemini Pro at Rs.325/device (14+ groups). ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro, Perplexity Pro, Midjourney, and Notion AI are entirely absent. Gemini has thin group density: 14 groups vs Netflix's 149.
Evidence & Analysis
ChatGPT Plus ~Rs.1,700/month. Perplexity Pro ~Rs.1,400/month. Midjourney ~Rs.830/month. Dollar-denominated and actively painful for Indian users. ChatGPT Plus shared via OpenAI Teams = Rs.425/device — a 75% saving. No OTT on Subspace achieves this. No Indian platform offers AI tool group sharing. First-mover window is open now.
Ship Instead
Dedicated "AI Tools" section in Explore. Initial: ChatGPT Plus, Perplexity Pro, Midjourney, Notion AI, Google One AI Premium. Lead with savings math. GTM: Partner with college tech communities and startup ecosystems. Viral hook: "Share ChatGPT with 4 friends for Rs.425/month."
05
UX
13 Undifferentiated Items in Account — Information Architecture Breakdown
UX
Observed
13 flat menu items with no grouping or sections: Profile, Add Address, Rental History, Order History, App Settings, Payout Methods, Friends, Blogs and Articles, AI Powered Mailbox, Money Saved, Refer & Earn, KYC Verification, App Info, Help & Support, Review Us.
Evidence & Analysis
KYC and Payout setup are the two most critical actions on the platform. Without KYC, users cannot withdraw earnings. Without Payout setup, admins cannot collect revenue. Both are buried alongside "Blogs and Articles" and "Review Us." This structurally suppresses KYC completion rate.
Ship Instead
Three labelled sections: "My Profile" (Profile, KYC, Address), "My Money" (Wallet, Payout, Orders, Rentals, Money Saved), "Community" (Friends, Refer & Earn, AI Mailbox). KYC and Payout promoted as inline progress cards at the top of Account — not buried list items.
